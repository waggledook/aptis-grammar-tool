# Listening Part 2: finding the right detail

## How to work

Your teacher will select from these eight independent extracts. Complete each gap with a word or two-word phrase from the recording or reading. Check your spelling. Every answer is a noun phrase.

Read the complete note and predict the information needed, rather than an exact word. Try an answer on the first listen. Use the second listen to check specific uncertainty. Mark uncertain answers with a question mark.

### Extract 1 — Designing public seating

The designers use **(1) __________** to make their first small models of the benches.

### Extract 2 — Organising volunteers

The shared calendar helps the volunteers avoid **(2) __________**.

### Extract 3 — Access to museum tours

Visitors can now join a live tour delivered in **(3) __________**.

### Extract 4 — Participation in evening classes

### Extract 5 — Improving a study centre

People studying on their own gave the highest priority to additional **(5) __________**.

### Extract 6 — A neighbourhood equipment scheme

### Extract 7 — Planning sheltered seating

The designers recorded **(7) __________** to decide the shelters' orientation.

The final layout was adjusted to keep **(8) __________** accessible.

### Extract 8 — Researching a night bus service

The research focused particularly on the travel needs of **(9) __________**.

The researchers chose **(10) __________** so they could explore issues raised by participants.
