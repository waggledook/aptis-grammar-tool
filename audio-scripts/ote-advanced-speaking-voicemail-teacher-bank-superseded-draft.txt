OTE Advanced Speaking Part 2 — Superseded Twelve-task Draft
============================================================

These are the twelve tasks previously drafted by Codex. They are preserved for reference only and are not wired into the current eight-task teacher bank.

1. Presentation Timetable Clash
-------------------------------

You study at college. Your tutor, Professor Lane, has scheduled your group presentation for Friday afternoon. You have just discovered that two members of your group have an important laboratory class at the same time. The presentation timetable is already very busy. Leave a voice message for your tutor and:

- explain the timetable problem
- acknowledge that changing the schedule may be difficult
- suggest a practical alternative

2. Double-booked Meeting Room
-----------------------------

You work for a company. Your colleague, Ben, has booked the main conference room for a team meeting tomorrow morning. You had already reserved the same room for an important client presentation, but the booking system did not save your reservation. Ben's meeting involves several people. Leave a voice message for Ben and:

- explain what has happened
- say why you need the room
- suggest a solution that could work for both meetings

3. Outdoor Volunteer Event
--------------------------

You volunteer for a local charity. The organiser, Sofia, has planned an outdoor fundraising event for Saturday. The weather forecast now predicts strong winds and heavy rain, but a large number of volunteers have already agreed to attend. Leave a voice message for Sofia and:

- acknowledge the work she has done
- explain your concern about the event
- suggest a safer alternative

4. Temporary Remote Working
---------------------------

You work for a company. Your manager, Mr Shah, expects everyone to work in the office next week because an important project is approaching its deadline. However, urgent repairs are being carried out in your home and you need to be there for two mornings. Leave a voice message for your manager and:

- show that you understand the needs of the project
- explain your situation
- propose an arrangement that will not delay your work

5. Dietary Requirements
-----------------------

You are helping to organise a college awards evening. Your colleague, Rachel, has chosen a catering company and wants to confirm the order today. You have noticed that the proposed menu does not include suitable options for several guests with dietary requirements. Rachel negotiated a good price with the company. Leave a voice message for Rachel and:

- recognise the work she has done
- explain why the menu may be a problem
- suggest how the issue could be resolved

6. Missing Research Credit
--------------------------

You study at college. Your project partner, Leo, has prepared the final slides for a presentation you will give together. The slides include research that you completed, but your contribution is not mentioned. Leo had very little time to finish the presentation. Leave a voice message for Leo and:

- thank him for preparing the slides
- explain your concern
- suggest a fair change before the presentation

7. Product Quality Check
------------------------

You work for a company. Your team leader, Nadia, wants to send a new product to an important customer tomorrow. During a final check, you found a minor fault that may affect the product's reliability. Fixing it could delay delivery by one day, and the customer is expecting the product on time. Leave a voice message for Nadia and:

- explain what you discovered
- say why sending the product may be risky
- recommend what the team should do

8. Accessible Workshop Materials
--------------------------------

You study at college. The library coordinator, Mr Green, is running a popular research workshop next week. All the places have been booked, but the digital materials are not compatible with the screen-reading software used by one student in your class. The coordinator has already prepared many documents. Leave a voice message for Mr Green and:

- explain the accessibility problem
- show that you understand the extra work involved
- suggest a practical way to help

9. Expensive Training Course
----------------------------

You work for a company. Your manager, Elena, has recommended an external training course for your team. The course would be useful, but you have learned that it costs much more than the remaining training budget. Elena believes the team needs the new skills urgently. Leave a voice message for Elena and:

- agree that the training would be valuable
- explain your concern about the cost
- suggest a more affordable solution

10. Guest Speaker Cancellation
------------------------------

You are a member of a college society. The chair, Aisha, has organised a public talk for tomorrow evening, but the guest speaker has just cancelled. Aisha wants to continue with the event because more than fifty people have registered. Leave a voice message for Aisha and:

- acknowledge why she wants the event to continue
- explain your concern
- suggest an alternative programme

11. Printer Removal Plan
------------------------

You work for a company. The office manager, Tom, plans to remove all shared printers next month to reduce paper waste. You support the environmental aim, but some staff regularly need printed documents for legal and accessibility reasons. The decision has already been announced. Leave a voice message for Tom and:

- show support for the aim of the plan
- explain why removing every printer could cause problems
- suggest a balanced alternative

12. Incomplete Group Report
---------------------------

You study at college. Your group leader, Chloe, wants to submit your project report this evening, one day before the deadline. You have noticed that an important section has not been checked and may contain incorrect information. Chloe wants to submit early because she will be unavailable tomorrow. Leave a voice message for Chloe and:

- recognise why she wants to submit the report today
- explain your concern about the unchecked section
- suggest how the group can meet the deadline safely
