# Intermediate Plus visual asset checklist

Audited 21 October 2026 against the 12 supplied vocabulary banks, the current Intermediate Plus vocabulary data, and existing Topic Vocab and textbook visuals. Candidate images were inspected visually as well as checked by filename. The Packing section now also records the supplied image intake and document images created in this chat; the other banks remain a production plan.

## How to use this list

- **Reuse** means an existing picture is suitable for the stated target. Use its current file rather than making another copy.
- **Contextual reuse** means the picture needs a sentence, a marked detail or a crop to make the intended answer clear. It should not be used as an unqualified picture-to-word question.
- **New** identifies gaps if we add the proposed picture activities. Lists name vocabulary targets, not necessarily separate image files: a scene with hotspots can cover several targets.
- **Optional** means useful enrichment, rather than a prerequisite for a sound activity.

Existing Topic Vocab is the first source; suitable visuals elsewhere in the project are also included. Paths below are relative to this document. New files could live under `public/images/vocab/intermediate-plus/<bank>/`. Shared images should have one source file, referenced by every relevant bank.

## 1. Adjective suffixes

**Reuse, optionally:** the Topic Vocab icons for [reliable](../public/images/vocab/people/reliable.png), [sensible](../public/images/vocab/people/sensible.png), [sensitive](../public/images/vocab/people/sensitive.png), [stressful](../public/images/vocab/work/stressful.png) and [spacious](../public/images/vocab/transport/spacious.png).

**New:** none essential. The suffix columns, contextual contrasts and sentence-based word formation already test the intended knowledge. Creating a picture for every abstract adjective would add little value. Existing icons can support introductory cards with a definition; a generic symbol alone will not reliably distinguish considerate, thoughtful, compassionate and helpful.

## 3. Shops and services

**Reuse:** [chemist's](../public/images/vocab/a2-places-buildings/chemist.webp) and the [try on](../public/images/vocab/clothes/try-on.png) action.

**Contextual reuse:** the existing [market](../public/images/vocab/a2-places-buildings/market.webp) can introduce the setting; a marked stall may support market stall. The [hairdresser](../public/images/vocab/jobs-a2/hairdresser.webp) introduces the profession, rather than unambiguously depicting a hairdresser's premises.

**New:**

- Premises: baker's, barber's, butcher's, car showroom, deli, DIY store, dry-cleaner's, estate agent's, fishmonger's, florist's, garden centre, greengrocer's, hairdresser's, jeweller's, launderette, market stall, newsagent's, off-licence, stationer's, travel agent's.
- Other venues: charity shop, pound shop, craft fair, outlet.
- Chain store: a short panel showing the same shop in different locations, rather than one anonymous storefront.
- Optional shopping scenes: a business closing down; an empty product shelf to support sell out / be out of; someone browsing for look round and someone seeking a particular item for look for. These distinctions still need contextual wording.

**Best visual activity:** a consistent shopping-street or storefront collection, with visible goods/services. Avoid making the shop sign itself supply the answer. Chain store and the shopping verbs work best with a short situation as well as a picture.

## 4. Photography

**Reuse:** [camera](../public/images/vocab/small-things/camera.webp), [photo](../public/images/vocab/small-things/photo.png), [take a photo](../public/images/vocab/more-verb-phrases-a2/take-a-photo.webp); existing simple position cards for [behind](../public/images/vocab/a2-prepositions/behind.webp), [in front of](../public/images/vocab/a2-prepositions/in-front-of.webp) and [opposite](../public/images/vocab/a2-prepositions/opposite.webp).

**Contextual reuse:** existing speaking pictures such as the [balcony scene](../public/images/speaking/describing-pictures/balcony-scene.png) and [mountain scene](../public/images/speaking/describing-pictures/mountain-scene.png) can support photo description. They were not composed to contrast every target position clearly.

**New:**

- Two purpose-built scenes with clearly identifiable subjects in the foreground, background, distance, centre and specified corners. Include behind / in front of relationships within the scenes.
- A paired diagram distinguishing on top of an object from at the top of a picture.
- A camera diagram or close view identifying lens, flash and settings, including portrait setting.
- Comparison pictures: sharp versus out of focus; normal exposure versus overexposed; a wider view versus zoom in / close-up; standard versus wide-angle view, using a landscape where appropriate.
- A simple photo-editing view for edit.

**Best visual activity:** hotspots and sentence descriptions of the same scene, then paired photography comparisons. Simple position overlays and interface diagrams can be drawn in code; they do not require generated artwork. Reuse the same underlying subject in comparisons so the photographic difference is what the learner notices.

**Contextual reuse:** the take-out picture contains a bin bag and an outdoor bin. Marking either detail can support those nouns; separate object pictures would be clearer for small flashcards.

**New:**

- Packaging: can, cardboard box, carton, jar, lid, cap, packet, plastic bag, pouch, sell-by date, tin, polystyrene tray, tub, wrapper.
- Waste vocabulary: an unambiguous bin, bin bag and waste-paper basket; refuse collector; landfill site; a rubbish/waste scene that can be reused in contextual prompts.
- Optional action scenes: give away/donate usable items; take away waste; recycle; reuse an object for a second purpose.

**Important distinctions:** use a drinks can versus a food tin; a milk/juice carton versus the existing milk bottle; a wide food tub versus the yoghurt pot; a flat lid versus a screw cap; a pouch versus a packet; and an outer wrapper clearly separated from its contents. The existing butter picture is a block on a dish, not a tub.

**Best visual activity:** a packaging collection for identification and comparison, plus a waste-disposal scene. Prefix re- formation can remain text-based; pictures of every re- verb are unnecessary. A sell-by-date close-up needs readable example text.

## Suggested production order and shared assets

1. **Object recognition:** fill the Packing, Packaging, Restaurant and DIY gaps. These support clear picture matching, flashcards and written naming, with substantial coverage from a manageable collection of object sets/scenes.
2. **Scene recognition:** countryside/farm features and shops. Build scenes and close-ups together so small-screen recognition remains possible.
3. **Action and contrast pictures:** hair/fitness/beauty, restaurant actions, and photography comparisons. These need more careful composition to avoid multiple valid answers.
4. **Small additions to existing collections:** five TV programme types. Study/application mock documents are optional; Adjective suffixes and most Phrasal verbs can remain text-first.

Maintain one reusable source for each shared concept: TV controls, shopping verbs, disposal/donation, restaurant send back, DIY assembly/disassembly and photography zoom. Packing's guidebook/first-aid kit and DIY's torch/batteries-related contexts can also draw on the same objects where they recur in later practice.

The production count should be decided after choosing scene/hotspot versus individual-card activities. A list of 20 target nouns does not automatically require 20 separately generated pictures. Record target words, existing file or new source, any focus region, and activity eligibility when wiring images into the vocabulary data; do not treat this checklist as an implemented asset registry.
