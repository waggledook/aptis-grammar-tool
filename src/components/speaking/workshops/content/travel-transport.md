# Aptis Speaking Topic Bank: Travel & Transport

## Part 1 — Personal questions

1. How do you usually travel around your town or city?
2. Tell me about a journey you make regularly.
3. What is your favourite way to travel?
4. How often do you use public transport?
5. Tell me about a place you enjoyed visiting.
6. Do you prefer travelling alone or with other people?
7. What kind of transport do you use most often?
8. Tell me about a long journey you have made.
9. Do you enjoy travelling to new places?
10. How do you usually get to work or school?
11. What do you normally do during a long journey?
12. Tell me about a journey you remember well.
13. Do you prefer travelling by car or by public transport?
14. Where would you like to travel in the future?
15. What is transport like where you live?

**Photo:** Passengers travelling on a train.

**Photo:** People travelling together by car.

1. Describe the photograph.

# Part 3 — Two photographs

## Task 1 — Public transport and private cars

**Photo A:** People travelling on a bus or train.
**Photo B:** Someone driving a car.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What kinds of journeys are these forms of transport best suited to?
3. Which way of travelling do you think is more convenient? Why?

## Task 2 — Train and plane

**Photo A:** Passengers travelling by train.
**Photo B:** Passengers travelling by plane.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. How might the experience of these journeys be different?
3. Which would you prefer for a journey of several hours? Why?

## Task 3 — Cycling and driving

**Photo A:** Someone cycling through a city.
**Photo B:** Someone driving through heavy traffic.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What difficulties might the travellers face in these situations?
3. Which is a better way to travel around a busy city? Why?

**Photo A:** Someone travelling alone on a train.
**Photo B:** Friends or family travelling together.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.

## Task 5 — City and country journeys

**Photo B:** A vehicle travelling along a quiet rural road.

# Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — A memorable journey

1. Tell me about a journey you remember particularly well.
2. What made this journey memorable for you?
3. What makes a journey enjoyable for most people?

## Task 2 — A delayed journey

1. Tell me about a time when a journey did not go as planned.
2. How did you deal with the problem?
3. Why do travel problems cause more stress for some people than others?

## Task 3 — Travelling somewhere new

1. Tell me about a time when you travelled somewhere for the first time.
2. What did you enjoy most about the experience?
3. Why do some people enjoy visiting unfamiliar places more than others?

## Task 4 — Travelling with other people

1. Tell me about a journey you made with friends or family.
2. What did you enjoy about travelling together?
3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of travelling with other people?

## Task 5 — Public transport

1. Tell me about a journey you made using public transport.
2. What was good or bad about the journey?
3. How important is good public transport for a town or city?

## Task 6 — A long journey

1. Tell me about a particularly long journey you have made.
2. How did you pass the time during the journey?
3. Why do some people cope with long journeys better than others?

## Task 7 — Visiting a favourite place

1. Tell me about a place you have enjoyed travelling to.
2. What made your visit there special?
3. Why do people often return to the same places when they travel?

## Task 8 — A difficult journey

1. Tell me about a journey that was more difficult than you expected.
2. What made the journey difficult?
3. What can people do to make difficult journeys less stressful?
