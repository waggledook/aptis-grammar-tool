# Home & Neighbourhood

## Part 1 — Familiar personal questions

1. What kind of home do you live in?
2. What do you like most about your home?
3. Which room do you spend most time in?
4. Tell me about the area where you live.
5. What is the best thing about your neighbourhood?
6. What shops or services near your home do you use most often?
7. Do you know many of your neighbours?
8. Is the area where you live usually quiet or busy?
9. How often do you use parks or green spaces near your home?
10. Tell me about the home you lived in when you were younger.
11. Is there anything you would like to change about your home?
12. Would you rather live near the centre of a city or further away?
13. What do you usually do when you spend an evening at home?
14. Has the area where you live changed since you first knew it?
15. Would you prefer to live in a house or a flat?

# Part 2 — One photograph

## Task 1 — Relaxing at home

**Photo:** One or two people relaxing in a comfortable living room.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. What do you usually do when you relax at home?
3. Why is having a comfortable home important to people?

## Task 2 — Working or studying at home

**Photo:** Someone working or studying at a desk in a home environment.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. What is your home like as a place to work or study?
3. What makes a home suitable for working or studying?

## Task 3 — A local park

**Photo:** People using a park or green space in a residential area.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. What green spaces are available near where you live?
3. Why are parks and green spaces important in residential areas?

## Task 4 — Local shops

**Photo:** People using small shops or other local businesses on a neighbourhood street.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. What local shops or services do you use regularly?
3. Why are local shops and services important for a neighbourhood?

## Task 5 — Neighbours

**Photo:** Neighbours talking outside their homes or in a shared residential area.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. What contact do you have with your neighbours?
3. What are the advantages of having a good relationship with your neighbours?

## Task 6 — Living in a flat

**Photo:** People entering or spending time outside a modern block of flats.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. What kind of housing is common where you live?
3. Why do many people choose to live in flats?

## Task 7 — Improving a home

**Photo:** Someone painting, decorating or making improvements to a room.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. Is there anything you would like to improve or change about your home?
3. Why do people spend time and money improving their homes?

## Task 8 — New housing

**Photo:** New homes being built in an established residential area.

1. Describe the photograph.
2. Has the area where you live changed much in recent years?
3. How can building new homes affect people who already live in an area?

# Part 3 — Two photographs

## Task 1 — House or flat

**Photo A:** People at a detached or semi-detached house.

**Photo B:** People at a modern apartment building.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What might be easier or more difficult about living in these two kinds of home?
3. Which kind of home would suit you better? Why?

## Task 2 — Central or quieter neighbourhood

**Photo A:** An attractive, lively residential area close to a city centre.

**Photo B:** A quieter residential neighbourhood further from the centre.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. How might people's daily lives be different in these two areas?
3. Which kind of lifestyle would you prefer? Why?

## Task 3 — Old and new neighbourhoods

**Photo A:** An established residential street with older traditional buildings.

**Photo B:** A recently built neighbourhood with modern housing.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What might residents value about living in these two neighbourhoods?
3. Would you rather live in an established neighbourhood or a new one? Why?

## Task 4 — Busy and quiet streets

**Photo A:** A lively neighbourhood street with shops, cafés and pedestrians.

**Photo B:** A quiet residential street with little traffic or commercial activity.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. How might the atmosphere of these streets affect the people who live there?
3. Which kind of street would you prefer to live on? Why?

## Task 5 — Garden and public park

**Photo A:** People enjoying a private garden at home.

**Photo B:** People relaxing in a public park in a residential area.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. How might people use these outdoor spaces differently?
3. Which kind of outdoor space would you value more near your home? Why?

## Task 6 — Large and small homes

**Photo A:** People in a large, spacious home.

**Photo B:** Someone in a smaller but comfortable home.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What might make these homes suitable for different people?
3. Which kind of home would suit your lifestyle better? Why?

## Task 7 — Local shops and a shopping centre

**Photo A:** People shopping in small local businesses on a neighbourhood street.

**Photo B:** People shopping in a modern indoor shopping centre.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What might people like about shopping in these two places?
3. Which kind of shopping is more useful for people in a neighbourhood? Why?

## Task 8 — Improving old homes or building new ones

**Photo A:** Workers renovating an older residential building.

**Photo B:** Workers constructing new homes.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. How might these two kinds of work affect people living nearby?
3. Do you think it is better to improve old homes or build new ones? Why?

# Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — A home you know well

1. Tell me about a home where you have spent a lot of time.
2. What do you particularly like or remember about it?
3. What makes somewhere feel like home?

## Task 2 — A local place

1. Tell me about a place near your home that you visit regularly.
2. Why is this place useful or enjoyable for you?
3. What makes local facilities valuable to a community?

## Task 3 — Enjoying time at home

1. Tell me about a time when you particularly enjoyed spending time at home.
2. What made the experience enjoyable?
3. Why do some people enjoy spending more time at home than others?

## Task 4 — A change in your area

1. Tell me about a change you have noticed in an area where you live or used to live.
2. How did the change affect the area or the people living there?
3. Do changes in a neighbourhood usually make life better for residents? Why or why not?

## Task 5 — A neighbour

1. Tell me about a neighbour you know or used to know.
2. How did you get to know this person?
3. How important is it for people to know their neighbours?

## Task 6 — A problem at home

1. Tell me about a problem you have had in your home.
2. How did you deal with the problem?
3. What kinds of problems at home can have the biggest effect on people's daily lives?

## Task 7 — Visitors at home

1. Tell me about a time when you had visitors at your home.
2. What did you do to make the visit enjoyable?
3. Why do some people enjoy inviting others into their homes?

## Task 8 — A favourite local area

1. Tell me about an area near your home where you enjoy spending time.
2. What do you particularly like about this area?
3. What makes a neighbourhood an enjoyable place to live?
