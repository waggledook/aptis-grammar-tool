# Education & Learning

## Part 1 — Familiar personal questions

1. What subject did you enjoy most at school?
2. Did you enjoy school when you were younger?
3. What are you learning at the moment?
4. Where do you usually study or learn new things?
5. Do you prefer studying alone or with other people?
6. Tell me about a teacher you remember well.
7. What subject did you find difficult at school?
8. Do you enjoy learning languages?
9. How do you usually prepare for a test or exam?
10. Have you ever taken an online course?
11. Do you prefer practical activities or studying from books?
12. What helps you concentrate when you are studying?
13. Is there a skill you would like to learn?
14. Do you think you are good at learning new things?
15. What makes a good teacher?

# Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — An enjoyable subject

1. Tell me about a subject you particularly enjoyed studying.
2. What made the subject enjoyable for you?
3. Why do people often enjoy some subjects more than others?

## Task 2 — Helping someone learn

1. Tell me about a time when you helped someone learn something.
2. What did you do to help them understand?
3. Why can teaching something to another person help people learn too?

## Task 3 — A memorable teacher

1. Tell me about a teacher you remember particularly well.
2. What made this teacher memorable?
3. What qualities make someone a good teacher?

## Task 4 — Preparing for an exam

1. Tell me about a time when you prepared for an important test or exam.
2. What did you do to prepare?
3. Why do some people find exams more stressful than others?

## Task 5 — Learning with other people

1. Tell me about a time when you learned something with other people.
2. How did the other people help the learning process?
3. What are the advantages of learning with others?

## Task 6 — Something difficult to learn

1. Tell me about something you found difficult to learn.
2. What helped you make progress?
3. Why do people sometimes give up when learning something difficult?

## Task 7 — An enjoyable course

1. Tell me about a course or class that you enjoyed.
2. What did you particularly like about it?
3. What makes people want to continue attending a course?

## Task 8 — Learning outside formal education

1. Tell me about something useful you learned outside school or a formal class.
2. How did you learn it?
3. Why is learning outside formal education important?
