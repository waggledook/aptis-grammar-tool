# Work & Careers

## Part 1 — Familiar personal questions

1. What kind of work do you do, or would you like to do?
2. What do you like most about your work or studies?
3. Do you prefer working alone or with other people?
4. What makes a good place to work?
5. Would you like to work from home?
6. Do you prefer a fixed routine or a varied working day?
7. Tell me about a job you had or would like to have.
8. What skills are important in your work or studies?
9. Would you like to work outdoors?
10. How important are friendly colleagues at work?
11. Do you prefer starting work early or finishing later?
12. Is there a job you would never like to do?
13. Would you like to run your own business?
14. What kind of job did you want when you were younger?
15. How important is free time outside work?

## Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — A job you know well

1. Tell me about a job or type of work that you know well.
2. What do you think is particularly good or difficult about it?
3. What makes people satisfied with their jobs?

## Task 2 — Working with other people

1. Tell me about a time when you worked with other people on a task.
2. What helped you work well together?
3. Why is teamwork important in many jobs?

## Task 3 — Starting something new

1. Tell me about a time when you started a new job, course or project.
2. What helped you get used to the new situation?
3. Why can starting something new at work or in education be difficult?

## Task 4 — Learning something for work

1. Tell me about something you had to learn for a job or practical task.
2. What helped you learn how to do it?
3. Why is it important for people to continue developing new skills during their careers?

## Task 5 — Solving a problem

1. Tell me about a problem you had to solve at work, while studying or during a project.
2. What did you do to solve it?
3. Why are problem-solving skills important at work?

## Task 6 — A change in your routine

1. Tell me about a time when your work or study routine changed.
2. How did the change affect you?
3. Why can changes at work be difficult for some people?

## Task 7 — Choosing a career

1. Tell me about a job or career that you have considered doing.
2. What attracted you to that kind of work?
3. What factors should people consider when choosing a career?

## Task 8 — Work and free time

1. Tell me about a time when you were particularly busy with work or study.
2. How did you make time for other parts of your life?
3. Why is a good balance between work and free time important?
