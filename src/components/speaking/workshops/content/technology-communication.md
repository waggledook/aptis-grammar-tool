# Technology & Communication

## Part 1 — Familiar personal questions

1. What technology do you use every day?
2. Which device do you use most often?
3. What do you usually use your phone for?
4. Tell me about a useful app or website you use.
5. Is there any technology you find difficult to use?
6. How often do you use technology when you are away from home?
7. Do you usually read news or information online?
8. What was the first piece of technology you remember using?
9. Do you prefer using a phone, tablet or computer?
10. What piece of technology would be difficult for you to live without?
11. Have you bought any new technology recently?
12. How often do you use technology to pay for things?
13. Do you enjoy trying new apps or devices?
14. Is there a piece of technology you would like to buy?
15. Do you think you spend too much time using screens?

# Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — A useful device

1. Tell me about a piece of technology that you use a lot.
2. What makes it particularly useful to you?
3. Why do some technologies become part of everyday life so quickly?

## Task 2 — Solving a technology problem

1. Tell me about a time when a piece of technology did not work properly.
2. How did you solve the problem?
3. Why can people become so frustrated when technology stops working?

## Task 3 — Learning to use new technology

1. Tell me about a time when you learned to use a new device, app or program.
2. What helped you learn how to use it?
3. What makes new technology easy or difficult for people to learn?

## Task 4 — Finding something online

1. Tell me about a time when you found some useful information online.
2. How did the information help you?
3. Why do people now rely so much on the internet for everyday information?

## Task 5 — Buying technology

1. Tell me about a piece of technology that you bought or received.
2. How useful has it been since then?
3. What influences people when they choose new technology?

## Task 6 — Using technology away from home

1. Tell me about a time when technology was particularly useful while you were away from home.
2. What did it help you to do?
3. How has mobile technology changed what people can do when they are travelling or moving around?

## Task 7 — An app or digital service

1. Tell me about an app or online service that you use regularly.
2. What makes it convenient or useful for you?
3. Why are more everyday services becoming digital?

## Task 8 — Spending less time with technology

1. Tell me about a time when you spent less time than usual using technology.
2. What did you do instead?
3. Do you think people would benefit from spending less time using screens? Why or why not?
