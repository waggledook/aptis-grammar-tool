1. What do you usually eat for breakfast?
2. What is your favourite meal of the day?
3. Do you enjoy cooking?
4. How often do you eat out?
5. What kind of food do you like most?
6. Is there any food you do not like?
7. Who usually cooks in your home?
8. Do you prefer eating at home or in restaurants?
9. What do you usually drink with meals?

# Part 3 — Two photographs

## Task 1 — Eating at home and eating out

**Photo A:** People eating a meal at home.

**Photo B:** People eating in a casual restaurant.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What might people enjoy about eating in these two situations?
3. Which do you prefer: eating at home or eating out? Why?

## Task 2 — Fresh and quick meals

**Photo A:** Someone preparing a meal with fresh ingredients.

**Photo B:** Someone preparing something quickly, such as a simple ready-made meal.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. Why might people choose these different ways of preparing food?
3. Which is more practical for you in everyday life? Why?

## Task 3 — Supermarket and market shopping

**Photo A:** Someone shopping for food in a supermarket.

**Photo B:** Someone buying food at an outdoor or covered market.

1. Tell me what you see in the two pictures.
2. What might shoppers like about buying food in these different places?

**Photo A:** Someone having breakfast comfortably at home.

# Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — A memorable meal

1. Tell me about a meal you remember particularly well.
2. What made that meal stand out for you?
3. Why do people remember some meals for a long time?

## Task 2 — Preparing food for someone

1. Tell me about a time when you prepared food for someone else.
2. What was the experience like for you?
3. Why do people often prepare food for other people?

## Task 3 — A restaurant or café

1. Tell me about a restaurant or café that you enjoyed visiting.
2. What did you particularly like about it?
3. What makes people return to the same restaurant or café?

## Task 4 — Trying a new food

1. Tell me about a time when you tried a food or dish for the first time.
2. What did you think of it?
3. Why are some people more willing than others to try unfamiliar food?

## Task 5 — Ordering food

1. Tell me about a time when you ordered food instead of preparing it yourself.
2. Why did you choose to order food on that occasion?
3. Why do people use takeaway and food-delivery services?

## Task 6 — A place to buy food

1. Tell me about a place where you regularly buy food.
2. What do you particularly like about shopping there?
3. What influences where people choose to buy their food?

## Task 7 — Changing an eating habit

1. Tell me about something you have changed about the way you eat.
2. Why did you make that change?
3. Why can changing eating habits be difficult?

## Task 8 — Sharing a meal

1. Tell me about a time when you shared a meal with other people.
2. What did you particularly enjoy about the experience?
3. Why is eating together important in many cultures?
