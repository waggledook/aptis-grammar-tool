# Sport & Entertainment

## Part 1 — Familiar personal questions

1. Do you enjoy playing sport?
2. What sport do you like watching?
3. How often do you exercise?
4. Do you prefer exercising alone or with other people?
5. What kind of films do you enjoy?
6. How often do you watch films or series at home?
7. What kind of music do you listen to?
8. Have you ever been to a live concert?
9. Do you enjoy playing games?
10. What do you usually do for entertainment at weekends?
11. Did you play much sport when you were younger?
12. Do you prefer watching sport live or on television?
13. Tell me about a film or programme you enjoyed recently.
14. Is there a sport or activity you would like to try?
15. Do you prefer active or relaxing ways of spending your free time?

## Part 4 — Extended response

## Task 1 — A sport or physical activity

1. Tell me about a sport or physical activity that you enjoy.
2. What do you particularly like about it?
3. Why do some people find it difficult to exercise regularly?

## Task 2 — Watching sport

1. Tell me about a sporting event or match that you enjoyed watching.
2. What made it enjoyable or memorable?
3. Why can people become so interested in watching sport?

## Task 3 — A film or series

1. Tell me about a film or television series that you really enjoyed.
2. What did you particularly like about it?
3. Why do some films or programmes become extremely popular?

## Task 4 — Music you enjoy

1. Tell me about a time when you listened to music that you really enjoyed.
2. What made the experience enjoyable?
3. Why is music important in many people's everyday lives?

## Task 5 — Trying a new activity

1. Tell me about a sport or leisure activity that you tried for the first time.
2. What was the experience like?
3. Why do some people enjoy trying new activities more than others?

## Task 6 — Entertainment with other people

1. Tell me about a time when you enjoyed some entertainment with other people.
2. What did you enjoy about sharing the experience?
3. Why are some forms of entertainment more enjoyable with other people?

## Task 7 — A favourite way to relax

1. Tell me about something you enjoy doing to relax in your free time.
2. What do you particularly enjoy about this activity?
3. Why is having enough leisure time important for people?

## Task 8 — A game you enjoyed

1. Tell me about a game you have enjoyed playing, either recently or when you were younger.
2. What made the game enjoyable for you?
3. Why do games appeal to people of different ages?
